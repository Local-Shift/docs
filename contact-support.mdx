If you need help with LocalShift, there are several ways to reach us. Choose the one that fits your situation and we will get you back on track.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@localshift.io">
    Send a message to [**support@localshift.io**](mailto:support@localshift.io). This is the best option for non-urgent questions, screenshots, or issues that need documentation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Call or text" icon="phone" href="tel:+14352911432">
    Reach us at **(435) 291-1432**. Use this for urgent issues or when you need a quick answer during business hours.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Include the right details">
    Tell us your dealership name, the email address you use to log in, and a clear description of what is happening. If something is not posting, mention the vehicle year, make, and model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a screenshot if it helps">
    A picture of the error message, the extension popup, or the dashboard screen often saves a back-and-forth email. Attach it directly to your email or text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Help Center first">
    The in-app **Help Center** and this documentation site cover most setup and troubleshooting topics. A quick search may solve your question immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For visual learners, our video tutorial library walks through common tasks step by step. You can find it at the video tutorials page linked in the Help Center.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Support hours may vary. Email is monitored throughout the day, and phone or text is best during standard business hours. If you reach voicemail, leave your dealership name and callback number and we will return your call promptly.
</Note>
