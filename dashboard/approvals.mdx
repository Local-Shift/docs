The **Approvals** page appears in your side navigation only when your dealership requires a manager to approve salespeople before they can start posting to Facebook Marketplace. If you do not see **Approvals**, your dealership has turned this setting off.

## How approvals work

When a salesperson connects their LocalShift Chrome extension and requests to post, they land in the **Pending** tab until an Admin takes action. You can approve them, reject them, or undo a rejection later.

## Reviewing pending requests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Approvals page">
    Sign in to [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) and select **Approvals** from the side navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Pending tab">
    Each row shows the salesperson's name, their location, and the date they requested access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Select **Approve** to let them start posting immediately. Select **Reject** if they should not have access; this removes their Marketplace license.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Undo a rejection

If you reject someone by mistake, switch to the **Rejected** tab and select **Undo**. This restores them to the Pending list so you can approve them later.

<Warning>
  Rejecting a user removes their Marketplace license. They will not be able to post until they are approved or request access again.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Use the search box at the top of the page to filter by salesperson name or location when you have many requests to review.
</Tip>
