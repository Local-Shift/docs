The **Inventory** page shows every vehicle pulled in from your dealership's inventory feed. You can browse the full catalog and use filters to find specific vehicles quickly.

## How to filter vehicles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inventory page">
    Sign in to [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) and select **Inventory** from the side navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the filter panel">
    The filter panel on the left lets you narrow results by make, model, year, price range, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between views">
    Toggle between vehicle listings and other content types using the menu inside the filter panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do from here

- Review vehicle details such as price, mileage, and photos
- See which vehicles are set to post and which are held back
- Switch to a specific salesperson's feed to see only their inventory

<Warning>
  Changes you make in the dashboard filter view do not change what posts to Facebook Marketplace. To control posting, use the **Inventory** tab in the LocalShift Chrome extension.
</Warning>

## Tips for large inventories

- Combine multiple filters at once to drill down fast.
- Clear all filters with the reset button when you want to start over.
- Use the search box to find a vehicle by stock number or VIN.

## Choose which feed price to use

Super admins can choose which price from a dealer's feed LocalShift syncs. LocalShift uses this price for Marketplace listings and every other channel. The **Inventory Price Source** settings only appear for locations whose inventory source is Homenet or DealersLink.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the location settings">
    Edit the location, then expand **Inventory Configuration** > **Inventory Price Source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the price">
    Under **Price Source**, choose the price to use, such as **Feed default**, **Internet price**, or **MSRP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which vehicles it applies to">
    Under **Apply To**, choose all vehicles, new vehicles only, or used vehicles only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the fallback">
    Turn **Fall back to the feed default when empty** on to use the feed's default price when the chosen price is missing. With it off, a vehicle without the chosen price syncs with no price and does not post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the location. LocalShift queues an inventory sync, and listings that are already live show as pending price updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Skip the first photos in the feed

If the first photos in your feed aren't ones you want on Marketplace, use the **Discard First X Images** location setting. It drops that many photos from the start of every vehicle's photo list. The first photo that remains becomes the vehicle's main photo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the location settings">
    Edit the location, then expand **Unbranded Mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of photos to drop">
    Enter a number in **Discard First X Images**. Enter `0` to keep every photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the location. The change takes effect on the next inventory sync, when LocalShift refreshes the vehicles and their photos.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The same section has a **Discard Last X Images** setting that drops photos from the end of each vehicle's photo list.
</Tip>
