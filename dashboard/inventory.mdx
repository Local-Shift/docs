The **Inventory** page shows every vehicle pulled in from your dealership's inventory feed. You can browse the full catalog and use filters to find specific vehicles quickly.

## How to filter vehicles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inventory page">
    Sign in to [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) and select **Inventory** from the side navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the filter panel">
    The filter panel on the left lets you narrow results by make, model, year, price range, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between views">
    Toggle between vehicle listings and other content types using the menu inside the filter panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do from here

- Review vehicle details such as price, mileage, and photos
- See which vehicles are set to post and which are held back
- Switch to a specific salesperson's feed to see only their inventory

<Warning>
  Changes you make in the dashboard filter view do not change what posts to Facebook Marketplace. To control posting, use the **Inventory** tab in the LocalShift Chrome extension.
</Warning>

## Tips for large inventories

- Combine multiple filters at once to drill down fast.
- Clear all filters with the reset button when you want to start over.
- Use the search box to find a vehicle by stock number or VIN.
