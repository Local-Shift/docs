The LocalShift web dashboard at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) is your central place to track performance, manage inventory, and oversee your team. What you see in the side navigation depends on your role.

## What each role sees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Admin (manager)">
    Admins see **Users**, **Inventory**, and **Dashboard** in the side navigation. You can manage salespeople, review the full vehicle catalog, and check Marketplace performance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Member (salesperson)">
    Members see **Inventory** only. You can browse and filter the vehicles you are responsible for posting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Approvals** appears in the side navigation only when your dealership requires a manager to approve salespeople before they can post listings.
</Note>

## Pages everyone sees

No matter your role, you always have access to:

- **Help Center** (this site)
- **What's New**
- **My Profile**

## The Dashboard page

When you open **Dashboard**, you see a snapshot of your Marketplace activity. This includes total views on your listings, how many leads you have received, and a leaderboard so you can compare performance across your team.

Use the filters at the top of the page to narrow results by date range or location.
