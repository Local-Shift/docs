The LocalShift web dashboard at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) is your central place to track performance, manage inventory, and oversee your team. What you see in the side navigation depends on your role.

## What each role sees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Admin (manager)">
    Admins see **Users**, **Inventory**, and **Dashboard** in the side navigation. You can manage salespeople, review the full vehicle catalog, and check Marketplace performance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Member (salesperson)">
    Members see **Inventory** only. You can browse and filter the vehicles you are responsible for posting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Approvals** appears in the side navigation only when your dealership requires a manager to approve salespeople before they can post listings.
</Note>

## Pages everyone sees

No matter your role, you always have access to:

- **Help Center** (this site)
- **What's New**
- **My Profile**

## The Dashboard page

When you open **Dashboard**, you see a snapshot of your Marketplace activity. This includes total views on your listings, how many leads you have received, and a leaderboard so you can compare performance across your team.

Use the filters at the top of the page to narrow results by date range or location.

The **Marketplace Leaderboard** header has three buttons:

- **How to train your sales team** opens the **LocalShift Marketplace Sales Playbook**, with listing tips, a day-by-day follow-up plan, and scripts you can copy.
- **Customize your post descriptions** opens **Customize Marketplace Listings**, where you choose a location and set up its Marketplace descriptions. See [Customize your post descriptions](/dashboard/profile#customize-your-post-descriptions).
- **See how many units you sold** opens **Upload Sales Attribution** (beta). Upload a CSV with the header row `First Name,Last Name,VIN`, or click **Download sample CSV** for a template.

Each row in the leaderboard shows a salesperson's active posts, total posts, and leads. It also includes:

- **Lead Response**: the share of leads the salesperson responded to, their average reply time, and an AI-graded response quality score from 1 to 5. Reply times of 3 hours or less show green, up to 6 hours show yellow, and longer show red.
- **# Sold**: the number of vehicles sales reps reported sold through Marketplace. **No response** means the rep did not submit the monthly sales survey.
- **Settings**: the gear icon opens that user's license settings.

Below the leaderboard, click **Download Leads Report** to download your Marketplace leads as a CSV. The **Totals** table sums active posts, total posts, leads, sales, and revenue. The right side of the page shows **Top Marketplace Performers** and **Sold Cars**.
