The **My Profile** page is where you view and update your own LocalShift account. Everyone, no matter their role, has access to this page from the side navigation.

## What you can do here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your account information">
    Your profile card shows your name, email, phone number, and Marketplace token. Select the edit icon if you need to update your name or phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Select the **Change Password** button, enter your current password, then type and confirm your new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up two-factor authentication">
    If your dealership requires it, or if you want extra security, use the two-factor authentication card to turn it on and choose how you want to receive codes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your Marketplace template">
    The **Account Settings** card lets you edit the header, footer, and body template that appears on your Facebook Marketplace listings. Save your changes when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Updating your Marketplace template changes the text that appears on future listings you post. It does not change listings that are already live.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you forget your password, you can also reset it from the sign-in page at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) by selecting **Forgot password**.
</Tip>
