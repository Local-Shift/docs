The **My Profile** page is where you view and update your own LocalShift account. Everyone, no matter their role, has access to this page from the side navigation.

## What you can do here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your account information">
    Your profile card shows your name, email, phone number, and Marketplace token. Select the edit icon if you need to update your name or phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Select the **Change Password** button, enter your current password, then type and confirm your new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up two-factor authentication">
    If your dealership requires it, or if you want extra security, use the two-factor authentication card to turn it on and choose how you want to receive codes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your Marketplace template">
    Open the **Marketplace Template** tab in the **Settings** card. Edit the **Description Header** and **Description Footer**, then click **Save Description** and wait for the "Template saved successfully" message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Updating your Marketplace template changes the text that appears on future listings you post. It does not change listings that are already live.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you forget your password, you can also reset it from the sign-in page at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) by selecting **Forgot password**.
</Tip>

## Customize your post descriptions

Sales reps and dealer admins reach the description editor from different places.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sales rep">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the editor">
        In the Chrome extension, open the **Home** tab and click **Customize your post descriptions**. This opens the **Marketplace Template** tab on your profile.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Edit the header and footer">
        Update the **Description Header** and **Description Footer** text.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save your changes">
        Click **Save Description** and wait for the "Template saved successfully" message.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    <Warning>
      Switching to the **Notification Settings** tab or leaving the page before you click **Save Description** discards your changes. **Reset to Defaults** saves the default template immediately.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dealer admin">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the editor">
        Go to **Dashboard**, find the **Marketplace Leaderboard**, and click **Customize your post descriptions**. Pick the location you want to update.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reorder vehicle details">
        Drag rows in the **Drag to Reorder** list to change the order of vehicle details. To reorder with the keyboard, click a row, press Space, then use the arrow keys. The **Select Attributes** buttons only turn details on or off.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save your changes">
        Click **Save Description** and wait for the "Template saved successfully" message.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
