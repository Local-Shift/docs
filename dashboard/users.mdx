The **Users** page is where Admins manage the salespeople on their team. You can invite new users, edit existing ones, and check who is actively posting to Facebook Marketplace.

## What you see on the Users page

The table shows one row per salesperson with columns for:

- **User** (name and email)
- **Position**
- **Role**
- **Locations** they are assigned to
- **Status** (active or archived)
- **Marketplace User** (whether they are connected to the extension)
- **Email Subscribed**

## Common actions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a new salesperson">
    Select the **Add User** button in the top right. Fill in their name, email, and role, then send the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend an invitation">
    If someone did not receive their invite email, hover over their row and select the envelope icon to resend it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive or unarchive a user">
    Select the trash or restore icon next to a user to remove or bring back their access. When you archive an active user with a Marketplace license, LocalShift queues all of their Marketplace posts for deletion by default. Uncheck the box in the confirmation dialog to keep their posts. The user must keep the Chrome extension installed and stay logged into Facebook for the posts to be removed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock a locked account">
    If a user is locked out after too many failed sign-in attempts, the status column shows **locked**. Select the unlock icon once you confirm it is really them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the **Active Users** and **Archived Users** tabs at the top of the page to switch between current and former team members.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Only Admins and higher roles can see the **Add User** button and manage team members. Members (salespeople) do not have access to this page.
</Note>
