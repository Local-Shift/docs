Your LocalShift extension posts to Facebook Marketplace through your own Facebook account. To keep everything running smoothly, you need to connect your account, secure it, and stay current with any policy updates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Credentials tab">
    Click the LocalShift icon in Chrome, then select the **Credentials** tab in the popup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your LocalShift token">
    Paste the token you received during onboarding into the **LocalShift Token** field. If your token is invalid, copy it again from your onboarding email and re-paste it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Check the box to agree to the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**. You must accept these before the extension can post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the best practices">
    If prompted, read the **Marketplace Best Practices** and check the box to confirm you understand them. This helps protect your account and avoid duplicate listings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Click **Turn on two-factor authentication** to open your Facebook security settings. Pick either an authentication app or text message, finish Facebook's setup, then return and check **I turned on two-factor authentication**. Facebook trusts accounts that have this enabled, and it is the most common way to prevent posting interruptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    From the **Help & Learning** menu on the **Home** tab, choose **Confirm Your Identity**. Facebook may ask sellers to verify who they are. Completing this early keeps your posting uninterrupted. Facebook handles the whole process and LocalShift never sees your ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept updated terms when prompted">
    If LocalShift updates its policies, an **Updated Policies** screen appears when you open the extension. Review the updated **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**, check the agreement box, and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start posting">
    Once every item on the install checklist is checked, click **Let's Get Posting!**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a genuine personal Facebook profile that you actively use. Facebook often flags new or posting-only accounts as inauthentic.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Do not post inventory to Marketplace manually while using LocalShift, and do not use Marketplace while the extension is posting. This can create duplicate listings and lead to Facebook flags or account restrictions.
</Warning>
