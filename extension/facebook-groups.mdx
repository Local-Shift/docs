The **Groups** tab in your LocalShift Chrome extension lets you choose which Facebook buy and sell groups receive your vehicle listings. You can pin your most important groups, favorite groups for automatic posting, or pick groups individually from the **Inventory** tab.

## How group posting works

LocalShift only lists vehicles to groups that have **Buy and Sell** enabled. Other group types do not allow marketplace listings, so they will not appear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    Click the LocalShift extension icon in Chrome, then select the **Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync your groups">
    Press **Sync Groups** to refresh the list of groups you belong to. Stay on the extension and avoid opening other Facebook tabs during the sync so it completes without interruption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Favorite groups for auto-posting">
    Click the heart icon next to any group to add it to your **Favorited Groups**. Every vehicle you list will automatically be posted to these groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin groups to the top">
    Click the pin icon to move a group to the top of your list. This makes it easier to find when you are choosing groups for a single vehicle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Favoriting a group sends every vehicle you list to that group. Some groups may remove you for posting too often or sharing content they consider irrelevant. Choose your favorited groups carefully.
</Warning>

## Post a single vehicle to specific groups

If you prefer to choose groups per vehicle, go to the **Inventory** tab, select a vehicle, and tap **Post to Facebook Groups**. You can search groups, check the ones you want, and save your choices for that vehicle only.

<Tip>
  If a sync pauses before it finishes, it will continue automatically the next time the extension runs. You can also press **Sync Groups** again to resume it immediately.
</Tip>
