The **Inventory** tab shows every vehicle assigned to you. You can search by name, stock number, or VIN, and filter by status: **Ready**, **Already Posted**, or **Ineligible**. This helps you quickly find the vehicle you want to manage.

## Choose what gets listed first

If you have a unit you know will sell, you can move it to the front of the line.

1. Find the vehicle in your inventory list.
2. Turn on the **Post first** toggle next to that vehicle.

When the extension runs your daily posts, prioritized vehicles go out before the rest.

## Turn off posting for a vehicle

If you do not want a specific vehicle posted to Marketplace for you, turn on the **Do not post** toggle. That vehicle will be skipped during your daily posting. You can turn it back off anytime to re-enable posting.

## Post to Facebook Groups

For vehicles that are ready to post, click **Choose FB Groups** to pick which Facebook Groups that vehicle should also be posted to. A small badge shows how many groups are already selected. Targeting the right groups can increase your leads.

<Note>
  If a vehicle is already posted, ineligible, or marked as do not post, the **Post first** toggle will be disabled. Hover over it to see the reason.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Vehicles with photos appear at the top of the list. Make sure your inventory feed includes images so your listings stand out.
</Tip>
