The LocalShift Chrome extension lives in your browser and does the work of posting your dealership's vehicles to Facebook Marketplace. Once it is set up, it runs in a minimized Facebook window using your own Facebook account so buyers can message you directly through Facebook Messenger.

Open the extension by clicking its icon in Chrome. You will see a popup with several tabs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Home">
    This is your main dashboard. It shows whether everything is healthy, how many posts you have left today, and a big button to **Start my daily posts now**. You can also jump to the **Inventory** tab to pick which vehicles go first, open your **Sales Playbook**, or post to Facebook Groups.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inventory">
    See every vehicle assigned to you. You can search by name, stock number, or VIN. Use the **Post first** toggle to move a vehicle to the top of the posting list, or turn on **Do not post** to skip a vehicle. You can also choose which Facebook Groups a vehicle should be posted to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Groups">
    Manage the Facebook Groups you want to post in. Targeting the right groups can increase your leads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Status">
    Check whether your extension is ready to post. If something needs attention, this tab tells you exactly what to fix.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credentials">
    Enter your LocalShift token, accept the terms, and turn on two-factor authentication for your Facebook account. This tab also shows the install checklist so you know what is still needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's New">
    See new tutorials and feature updates from the LocalShift team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Playbook">
    Open your sales playbook for tips on selling more units through Marketplace.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Keep your computer open and stay off Facebook while the extension is running daily posts. Interacting with Facebook during posting can interrupt the process.
</Note>
