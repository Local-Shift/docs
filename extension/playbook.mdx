The **Playbook** and **What's New** tabs in your LocalShift Chrome extension help you sell more vehicles and stay up to date with new tools.

## Sales Playbook

The Playbook is a built-in guide with proven tips and scripts. It is organized into four sections you can step through at your own pace.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Consistency">
    List three vehicles every day. Keep Chrome running each morning and stay logged into your personal Facebook profile. Good photos and clean descriptions build trust, so avoid heavy dealership branding and keep the focus on the vehicle. Do not add phone numbers or links in descriptions, because Facebook may remove them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Follow Up">
    A five-day follow-up plan keeps leads warm even if they do not reply right away. Day one is an intro video. Day two asks if they saw it. Day three offers more photos or another video. Day four adds humor or mentions a mutual friend. Day five sends a lighthearted GIF. The goal is to get them into the dealership, not to close the deal over Messenger.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What to Say">
    This section gives you word-for-word follow-up messages you can copy and paste. Each day has its own script. Tap **Copy script** to save it to your clipboard and paste it directly into Facebook Messenger.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="FB Groups">
    Join local buy, sell, and trade groups in your area, then use the extension to post listings there. Review your group performance each month and keep only the ones that bring leads. After a sale, ask the buyer for a five-star review on your Facebook profile. Leave them a review first, then ask. A strong seller rating helps you rank higher and sell more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What's New

The **What's New** tab shows interactive tutorials that walk you through new features inside the extension. Each tutorial highlights buttons and settings step by step so you can learn by doing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse tutorials">
    Open the **What's New** tab to see the list of available tutorials. Use the search bar to find a specific topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a tutorial">
    Tap **Start** on any tutorial. The extension may switch to the right tab automatically, then highlight each step with on-screen pointers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow along">
    Use the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to move through the steps. You can skip or close a tutorial at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete it">
    When you finish a tutorial, it is marked as completed so you know which ones you have already done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Revisit the Playbook anytime you want a refresher on follow-up scripts or group strategy. It is available inside the extension whenever you need it.
</Tip>
