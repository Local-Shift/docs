The **Status** tab in your LocalShift Chrome extension shows whether everything is working or if something needs your attention. When all checks pass, you will see **All Systems Go**. If anything is wrong, the tab lists the exact issue and gives you a quick way to fix it.

## What the statuses mean

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="All Systems Go">
    Everything is running smoothly and your extension is ready to post listings. No action is needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Action Required">
    One or more checks failed. The Status tab lists each issue in red. Tap any item to go directly to the fix, such as logging back into Facebook or switching to your personal profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common warnings and how to fix them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Need to enter a valid token">
    Your extension token is missing or expired. Tap the item to open the **Credentials** tab and enter a fresh token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need to log back into Facebook">
    Your Facebook session ended. Tap the item to open Facebook and log in again with your personal profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need to switch from business profile to personal profile">
    You are currently on a Facebook business profile. Switch to your personal profile so LocalShift can post listings for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity with Facebook">
    Facebook is asking you to verify your account. Tap the item to open the identity confirmation page and follow the prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on two-factor authentication in Facebook">
    Facebook recommends two-factor authentication for security. Tap the item to open the **Credentials** tab and follow the steps to enable it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need to switch to English (US)">
    Your Facebook language is set to something other than English (US). Change it in Facebook settings so LocalShift can read pages correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need to enter PIN to see leads">
    Facebook is asking for a PIN to unlock your Marketplace messages. Tap the item to open Marketplace, enter your PIN, and then return to the extension.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A lot of your inventory is missing information needed for posting">
    Many vehicles are missing photos, price, or other required details. Check your inventory feed or contact your manager to update the missing information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Status tab checks your setup in real time. If you fix an issue and the warning does not disappear right away, give it a minute and then reopen the extension.
</Note>
