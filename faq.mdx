Here are the most common questions dealerships ask about LocalShift. If you need more detail, visit the related help articles or contact support.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does inventory sync work?">
    LocalShift reads your dealership inventory feed automatically. When new vehicles arrive, prices change, or vehicles sell, the system updates your Facebook Marketplace listings to match. You do not need to post or edit listings by hand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do buyers reach our sales team?">
    Shoppers on Facebook Marketplace click **Message** and start a conversation in Facebook Messenger. The message goes to the salesperson whose account posted the vehicle. Your team replies directly in Messenger, just like any other lead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How fast does inventory go live?">
    New vehicles usually appear on Facebook Marketplace within minutes after they show up in your feed. If your dealership requires approvals before posting, the vehicle waits until a manager approves it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when a vehicle sells?">
    Once the vehicle is marked sold in your inventory feed, LocalShift removes the Marketplace listing automatically. Buyers will no longer see it or be able to message you about it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does my team need to change how they work?">
    No. Salespeople keep using their normal Facebook accounts and Messenger apps. The only new habit is keeping the LocalShift Chrome extension running so listings stay active and synced.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stop a vehicle from posting?">
    Yes. If your dealership uses approvals, a manager can decline a vehicle before it goes live. You can also contact support to set rules about which vehicles should or should not post.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I prioritize a vehicle?">
    LocalShift posts vehicles in the order they appear in your feed. If you need a specific vehicle to go live faster, make sure it is active and approved in your inventory system. For custom prioritization rules, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
