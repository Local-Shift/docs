The LocalShift Chrome extension is what posts your dealership's vehicles to Facebook Marketplace using your own Facebook account. You install it once, connect it with your token, and it runs quietly in a minimized window while you work.

## Before you start

Make sure you are using Google Chrome on a computer. The extension does not run on mobile browsers or other desktop browsers.

## Install the extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the install link">
    Your LocalShift rep or onboarding email will send you the direct link to the extension. Click that link to open the Chrome Web Store page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Chrome">
    Click **Add to Chrome** and confirm the permission prompt. The extension will download and install automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it to your toolbar">
    Click the puzzle piece icon in the top right of Chrome, find LocalShift, and click the pin icon so it stays visible in your toolbar. This makes it easy to open the popup when you need it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Chrome open">
    The extension needs Chrome to stay open to post listings and stay in sync. You can minimize the window, but do not fully close the browser while you want vehicles to post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you do not see the install link, check your onboarding email or ask your manager for the Chrome Web Store URL.
</Note>

## What happens next

After installing, open the extension popup and go to the **Credentials** tab to enter your token and connect your Facebook account. See [Log in and connect the extension](/get-started/log-in) for the full walkthrough.
