You log in to LocalShift through the web dashboard at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io). After signing in, you connect the Chrome extension so it can post vehicles to Facebook Marketplace on your behalf.

## Log in to the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-in page">
    Open [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your login method">
    You can sign in with your email and password, or with your dashboard access code. Use whichever option your dealership set up for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password if needed">
    If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password** and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link by email. Follow that link to choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect the Chrome extension

Once you are logged in to the dashboard, link the extension so it can use your Facebook account to post listings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extension popup">
    Click the LocalShift icon in your Chrome toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the setup screen">
    The first time you open the extension, it shows a setup screen with a guided tour and an **Install Checklist**. The checklist tracks each step below and disappears once every item is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Marketplace best practices">
    Read the **Marketplace Best Practices** card and tick its checkbox to confirm you have read it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Check the box to agree to the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**. The **LocalShift Token** field unlocks after you finish these steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your token">
    Type or paste the token from your LocalShift account into the **LocalShift Token** field. This connects the extension to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure your Facebook account">
    In the **Secure your Facebook account** section, LocalShift recommends turning on two-factor authentication in Facebook. Click **Turn on two-factor authentication** to open Facebook's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start posting">
    Click **Let's Get Posting!** to finish setup. The extension is ready to post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are not sure where to find your token, look in your onboarding email or ask your manager. The token is what tells LocalShift which account and dealership the extension belongs to. To change your token later, click **Settings** at the bottom of the extension's **Home** tab.
</Tip>
