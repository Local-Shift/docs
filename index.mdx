LocalShift automatically lists your dealership's vehicle inventory on Facebook Marketplace and keeps everything in sync. New vehicles get posted, price changes are updated, and sold vehicles are removed without you lifting a finger. When buyers are interested, they message your sales team directly through Facebook Messenger.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your inventory">
    Your dealership's inventory feed is linked to LocalShift. That means your current stock flows in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We manage everything">
    LocalShift posts your vehicles, updates prices, and removes sold units. You do not need to create or edit listings by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leads go straight to your team">
    Interested buyers message your sales reps directly on Facebook Messenger. Your team responds just like any other lead.
  </Step>
</Steps>
