When a shopper sees your vehicle on Facebook Marketplace, they can message your dealership directly through Facebook Messenger. Those messages arrive right in the Messenger inbox tied to the salesperson whose account posted the listing. That means your sales team gets warm leads without any extra tools or logins.

Speed matters. Buyers often message multiple dealerships at once, and the first helpful response usually wins the conversation. Treat Messenger like any other lead channel: answer promptly, answer fully, and move the conversation toward an appointment or phone call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Messenger regularly">
    Open Facebook Messenger on your phone, desktop, or browser throughout the day. New messages appear as normal chat notifications from Marketplace buyers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply with the info they asked for">
    If a buyer asks about price, availability, or features, answer directly and offer the next step. For example: "Yes, this SUV is still available. Would you like to schedule a test drive this afternoon?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the conversation off Messenger when ready">
    Once the buyer is engaged, suggest a phone call, text, or in-person visit. Share your dealership phone number and a convenient time to connect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set Messenger notifications on your phone so you never miss a lead. Even a quick "Thanks for reaching out. I will get you an answer in five minutes" keeps the buyer interested.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Do not share personal payment apps or ask buyers to send deposits through Messenger. Keep payments and paperwork inside your dealership's normal sales process.
</Warning>

If your team uses a shared inbox or BDC process, decide who monitors Messenger and how handoffs work. The goal is simple: every Marketplace message gets a fast, friendly, useful reply.
