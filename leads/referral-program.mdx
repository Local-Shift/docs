You can earn a bonus for every dealership you refer to LocalShift. When a referred dealership starts posting inventory, you receive a payout. There is no limit to how many dealerships you can refer.

You can share your referral link from two places: the **Referral Program** page in the web dashboard and the **refer-and-earn** button inside the Chrome extension. Both give you the same personal link, so choose whichever is easier in the moment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your referral link">
    In the dashboard, open **Referral Program** and click **Copy Link**. In the extension, open the popup and click the refer-and-earn button, then copy the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with other dealerships">
    Send the link by email, text, or social media. The link tracks the referral back to you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the review">
    Our team reviews each referral manually. You will be notified when the referred dealership completes its first post and your payout is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Dealerships that are part of the same auto group as your store are not eligible for referral payouts.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I know if my referral signed up?">
    The Referral Program page shows the status of your referrals. You can see when a lead is contacted, when a demo is scheduled, and when the dealership goes live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do I get paid?">
    Payout is due once the referred dealership has its first post on Facebook Marketplace. You do not need to do anything extra; the system tracks it automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my link anywhere?">
    Yes. Your link is public and safe to share in emails, texts, LinkedIn messages, or at industry events. Just make sure the recipient is a decision maker at the dealership.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
