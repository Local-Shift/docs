This guide walks you through your first setup as a salesperson. Once you finish these steps, your vehicles will start posting to Facebook Marketplace automatically and leads will arrive in Facebook Messenger.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the dashboard">
    Go to [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io) and sign in with your email and password or your dashboard access code. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password** and follow the reset link in your email. For more details, see [Log in to the dashboard](/get-started/log-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Chrome extension">
    Your LocalShift rep or onboarding email will send you the install link for the LocalShift Chrome extension. Add it to Chrome and pin it to your toolbar so it is easy to reach. Keep Chrome open while you work. For full instructions, see [Install the extension](/get-started/install-extension).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the extension in Credentials">
    Open the extension popup and go to the **Credentials** tab. Enter the token from your LocalShift account to connect the extension. This links your Facebook account to LocalShift so listings can be posted on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review best practices and 2FA guidance">
    The extension will prompt you to accept terms, review best practices, and read the two-factor authentication guidance. This helps keep your account secure and your listings compliant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    Follow the on-screen steps to confirm your identity. Once that is done, the extension is fully connected and ready to post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Status tab">
    Open the **Status** tab in the extension popup to make sure everything is running smoothly. You should see that your connection is active and your inventory is ready to post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to leads">
    When buyers message you on Facebook Messenger, reply just like any other lead. LocalShift keeps your listings in sync so you can focus on selling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your dealership requires manager approval before you post, the **Approvals** section will appear in your dashboard. Ask your manager to approve you so your listings can go live.
</Tip>
