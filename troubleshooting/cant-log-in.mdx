If you cannot log in to LocalShift at [app.localshift.io](https://app.localshift.io), the cause is usually your credentials, your access method, or a browser issue. Try the fixes below to get back into your account.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You forgot your password">
    It happens to everyone. If you cannot remember your password, you can reset it by email.

    **Fix:** On the login page, click **Forgot password**. Enter your email address and check your inbox for a reset link. If you do not see the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. Click the link, create a new password, and try logging in again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your email or password is entered incorrectly">
    A small typo in your email or password will block access.

    **Fix:** Double-check that your email has no extra spaces and that Caps Lock is off. If you are unsure of the password, use the **Forgot password** link to reset it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your dashboard access code is not working">
    Some dealerships use a dashboard access code as a quick login option.

    **Fix:** Make sure you are entering the full code exactly as provided, with no extra spaces. If the code was changed recently, ask your manager for the current one. If it still does not work, switch to email and password to log in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your browser is blocking the login">
    Privacy extensions, ad blockers, or strict cookie settings can sometimes interfere with the login page.

    **Fix:** Try logging in from an incognito or private window. If that works, clear your browser cache and cookies for app.localshift.io, then try again in a normal window. You can also try a different browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your account has not been activated or approved">
    New accounts or accounts at dealerships with approvals turned on may need manager sign-off before you can log in.

    **Fix:** Ask your manager to check the **Users** or **Approvals** section on the dashboard. Once your account is activated or approved, you will be able to log in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You see an error page after logging in">
    If the login succeeds but you land on an error screen, there may be a temporary service issue.

    **Fix:** Wait a few minutes and try again. If the error continues, take a screenshot and [contact support](/contact-support) with the details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If none of these steps get you logged in, [contact support](/contact-support) for help.
