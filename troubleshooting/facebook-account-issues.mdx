---

If Facebook has locked your account, placed a restriction, or asked you to verify your identity, LocalShift cannot post listings until the issue is resolved. Facebook controls these limits, so the fix happens on their side first.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Facebook asked for a photo or ID verification">
    Facebook sometimes requests a photo of your ID or a selfie to confirm you are a real person.

    **Fix:** Follow the on-screen prompts in Facebook to upload the requested document. Verification can take a few hours to a few days. Once Facebook approves you, log back in and check that the extension **Status** tab shows connected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Facebook temporarily restricted Marketplace posting">
    Posting too quickly or unusual activity can trigger a temporary Marketplace hold.

    **Fix:** Wait out the restriction period, which is usually 24 to 72 hours. Avoid manually posting vehicles during this time. When the hold lifts, the extension will resume posting automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Facebook says the account is locked for security">
    A login from a new device or location can cause Facebook to lock the account for safety.

    **Fix:** Go to Facebook on the same computer where the extension runs and follow the security check steps. You may need to confirm your email or phone number. Once unlocked, check the extension to confirm it reconnects.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two-factor authentication is required">
    Facebook may require two-factor authentication before allowing Marketplace access.

    **Fix:** Enable two-factor authentication in your Facebook settings if it is not already on. The extension's setup flow also includes two-factor authentication guidance. After enabling it, log in again and confirm the extension status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Facebook removed access to Marketplace entirely">
    In rare cases, Facebook can revoke Marketplace access for a specific account.

    **Fix:** Visit the Facebook Help Center to appeal the decision. While the appeal is in progress, another salesperson with active Marketplace access can continue posting through LocalShift. Ask your manager about adding another user if needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If your Facebook account is clear but LocalShift still cannot post, [contact support](/contact-support) so the team can look into the extension side.
