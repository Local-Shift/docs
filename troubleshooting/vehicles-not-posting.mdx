If your vehicles are not showing up on Facebook Marketplace, the issue usually comes down to the Chrome extension, your account status, or your inventory feed. Work through the symptoms below to find the cause and fix.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Chrome is closed or your computer is asleep">
    The LocalShift extension only runs while Chrome is open on your computer. If Chrome is closed or your computer goes to sleep, posting pauses until you return.

    **Fix:** Keep Chrome open and your computer awake during business hours. If you step away, the extension will resume automatically when you come back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The extension is not connected">
    The extension needs an active connection to your LocalShift account. If the connection drops, new and updated vehicles will not post.

    **Fix:** Open the extension, go to the **Credentials** tab, and make sure your token is entered. Then check the **Status** tab to confirm it shows connected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Approval is pending">
    Some dealerships require a manager to approve salespeople before they can post. If your account is pending approval, your vehicles will not go live.

    **Fix:** Ask your manager to check the **Approvals** section on the dashboard. Once you are approved, posting will begin automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Terms have not been accepted">
    The extension asks you to accept terms before you can post. If this step was skipped, posting is blocked.

    **Fix:** Open the extension and look for a prompt to accept terms. Follow the steps, review the best practices and two-factor authentication guidance, and confirm your identity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Do not post' is turned on">
    In the extension's **Inventory** tab, each vehicle has a toggle. If **Do not post** is on, that vehicle is skipped.

    **Fix:** Open the **Inventory** tab, find the vehicle, and switch the toggle to **Post**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Facebook has restricted your account">
    Facebook may place temporary limits or request verification on your account. When this happens, LocalShift cannot publish listings.

    **Fix:** Check your Facebook account for alerts or verification requests. Once Facebook removes the restriction, the extension will resume posting. For more help, see our guide on [Facebook account issues](/troubleshooting/facebook-account-issues).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The vehicle is missing from the inventory feed">
    LocalShift pulls vehicles from your dealership's inventory feed. If a vehicle is not in the feed, it cannot be posted.

    **Fix:** Check your inventory feed or ask your manager to confirm the vehicle is included. Once it appears in the feed, LocalShift will sync it automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If you have checked the items above and your vehicles still are not posting, [contact support](/contact-support) for help.
